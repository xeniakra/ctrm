# Retracted change

**Special:** A later message proposes new quantity and dates, then explicitly withdraws that
message without restating the original numbers.

**Perturbations:** Timestamped group chat, lowercase amendment, uppercase retraction, em dash,
and trader shorthand `wrong stem`.

**Failure caught:** Blindly selecting the last numeric mention instead of resolving the retraction,
or treating the withdrawn amendment as unresolved ambiguity.
