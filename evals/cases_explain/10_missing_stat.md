# Missing statistic

**Special:** The quote, B/L-relative period, and premium are explicit, but no quotation statistic
is stated.

**Perturbations:** Contract-instruction register, em-dash labels, ordinal dates, numbers in words,
and a pricing clause wrapped across lines.

**Failure caught:** Defaulting to `mean`, confusing the quote's published mean column with an
agreed statistic, or treating line wrapping as a clause boundary.
