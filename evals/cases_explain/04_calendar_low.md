# Calendar low

**Special:** A polished contract email containing DAP delivery, a calendar-date pricing period,
and the `low` quotation statistic. It also includes an optional quality specification.

**Perturbations:** Formal register, Unicode `±`, en-dash date ranges, and dates written with month names.

**Failure caught:** Treating every pricing period as B/L-relative, confusing `low` with `mean`,
failing to resolve the spaced `ULSD 10` catalog-code alias, leaving `EN 590` bundled into the
product wording, dropping the named DAP place, or asking questions for optional fields that are
simply absent.
