# Forwarded amendments

**Special:** A complete deal is embedded in a forwarded email thread with later
amendments. The latest operative delivery dates and inspection terms must win
while superseded wording remains available as provenance.

**Perturbations:** Forwarding prefixes, quoted history, corrections, a separate
deal distractor, BBL quantity priced in USD/MT, `mean_of_high`, a positive
differential, EUR deal currency, fixed USD/EUR FX, and split provisional/final
payment terms.

**Failure caught:** Taking the oldest term instead of the amendment, merging the
distractor deal, confusing BBL with MT, averaging the wrong quote column,
dropping FX or differential, or conflating gross provisional cargo value with
the 90% provisional payment.
