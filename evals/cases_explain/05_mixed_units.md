# Mixed units

**Special:** The quantity is in barrels while the differential is per metric tonne. The product's
reference unit must not tempt the extractor to convert or overwrite either unit.

**Perturbations:** WhatsApp tone, compressed labels, `300k`, `pct`, `SO`, `px`, `ccy`, `vsl`,
ASCII `+/-`, and an en-dash laycan.

**Failure caught:** Premature unit conversion, misreading `SO`, losing the nominated vessel,
discarding a catalog product when its name also occurs in the pricing clause, retaining a laycan
as a note after structuring it, or preserving a non-canonical pricing-period expression.
