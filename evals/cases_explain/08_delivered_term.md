# Delivered term

**Special:** The named destination is clear, but `delivered Rotterdam` is explicitly not an
Incoterm under the field rules.

**Perturbations:** SMS prose, curly quotation marks, compact sentences, Unicode `±`, and a
natural-language tolerance option.

**Failure caught:** Guessing CFR or DAP from trader shorthand, or discarding Rotterdam when
abstaining on the Incoterm itself.
