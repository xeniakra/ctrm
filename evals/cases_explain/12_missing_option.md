# Missing option

**Special:** Quantity and tolerance percentage are exact, but the party controlling the tolerance
is not recorded. A null option is therefore an abstention, not a harmless optional value.

**Perturbations:** Broker blotter layout, uppercase shorthand, aligned columns, Unicode punctuation,
slash dates, and `$` instead of `USD` in the pricing clause.

**Failure caught:** Inferring seller's option from convention or silently accepting an unusable
mandatory field.
