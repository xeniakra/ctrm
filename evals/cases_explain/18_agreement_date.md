# Agreement date

**Special:** Three dates are present: proposal, counterparty confirmation, and forwarding. The recap
becomes agreed on the confirmation date.

**Perturbations:** Nested email quoting, multiple time zones, `FW`/`RE` headers, quoted-line prefixes,
and a terse confirmation that inherits all commercial terms.

**Failure caught:** Choosing the newest email timestamp or the proposal date, stripping quoted text,
or failing to combine the confirmation with the original recap.
