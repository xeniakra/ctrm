# Clean Telegram recap

**Special:** The clean baseline contains every mandatory field and several
optional operational terms in a compact Telegram-style message.

**Perturbations:** Trader abbreviations, signed negative differential,
B/L-relative pricing, percentage tolerance, payment timing, and optional legal,
inspection, and demurrage clauses appear without a formal document structure.

**Failure caught:** Missing an otherwise explicit mandatory value, losing the
negative differential sign, treating the laycan as a B/L date, resolving the
product without reference provenance, or asking unnecessary clarification
questions for a complete recap.
