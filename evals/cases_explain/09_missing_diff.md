# Missing differential

**Special:** Quote, statistic, and pricing dates are firm, while the differential is explicitly TBC.

**Source-led correction (10 September 2026):** Keep “Differential: TBC after today's call”
in notes: the missing-field questions do not preserve the follow-up timing. This does not
establish either a differential value or a unit.

**Perturbations:** Slack-style header, pipe separator, mixed date formats, `plus/minus`, and a
standalone follow-up line for the missing value.

**Failure caught:** Inventing zero or an industry-typical differential, or inferring its unit from
the quotation's unit.
